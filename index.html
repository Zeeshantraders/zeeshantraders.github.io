About Us

Your Reliable Partner for Lifting Equipment

Zeeshan Traders is a Lahore-based company specialising in the import, supply, installation, repair and maintenance of cranes, hoists, winches, wire rope and all types of lifting equipment.

As a registered importer, we bring lifting machinery, spare parts and rigging hardware directly from manufacturers abroad, which lets us offer quality equipment at competitive prices and supply items that are not easily available in the local market.

We work with government departments and public sector organisations through tenders, and with private industries that need dependable lifting solutions. Along with lifting equipment, we handle general order supply for a wide range of items.

Our strength is a large repair capacity: we restore and service existing equipment so our clients save time and cost.

What We Do

Our Services

From new supply and installation to repair of existing equipment, we cover the complete life of your lifting machinery.

01

Supply of New Equipment

Supply of cranes, hoists, winches, wire rope and lifting accessories as per client and tender specifications.

02

Import of Equipment & Spares

As a registered importer, we import lifting machinery and spare parts directly from foreign manufacturers.

03

Installation & Commissioning

Erection, installation and commissioning of overhead, gantry and jib cranes at your site.

04

Repair & Overhauling

Complete repair and overhauling of cranes, hoists and winches, mechanical and electrical, with large repair capacity.

05

Maintenance Contracts

Preventive maintenance and annual maintenance contracts to avoid breakdowns and keep equipment safe.

06

Electrical Works

Motor rewinding, control panels, VFD installation, pendant and radio remote control fitting.

07

Load Testing & Inspection

Load testing and inspection of cranes, hoists, slings and lifting gear.

08

Modernisation of Old Cranes

Upgrading old cranes with new hoists, electricals and safety devices to extend their working life.

09

Wire Rope Replacement

Replacement of worn wire ropes and fabrication of wire rope slings to required length and capacity.

10

Government Tender & General Order Supply

Supply of lifting equipment and general items to government departments and public sector organisations.
