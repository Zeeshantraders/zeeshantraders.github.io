About Overhead / EOT Cranes

An Electric Overhead Travelling (EOT) crane runs on rails fixed along the length of a building. A bridge girder spans the bay, a hoist travels across the girder, and the whole crane travels along the rails, so a load can be lifted and placed anywhere inside the covered area.

EOT cranes are the backbone of material handling in steel structures, engineering workshops, power stations and warehouses. They save floor space because they run overhead, and they move heavy loads quickly and safely without forklifts or manual effort.

Zeeshan Traders supplies complete EOT cranes as well as crane kits (end carriages, hoist and electricals) for fabrication of the girder locally. We also take up installation, load testing, repair and modernisation of existing cranes.

Range

Types We Supply

Single Girder EOT Crane

One main girder with an underslung hoist. Economical, light on the building structure and ideal for loads up to about 10 to 12 ton.

Double Girder EOT Crane

Two girders with a crab or open winch trolley running on top. Gives higher hook height and is suited for heavy loads and long spans.

Underslung / Under-hung Crane

Runs on the bottom flange of runway beams hung from the roof structure. Useful where columns cannot carry a gantry rail.

Top Running Bridge Crane

End carriages run on rails mounted on top of the runway beams. The standard choice for medium and heavy duty work.

Hot Shed & Workshop Cranes

Cranes with heat-resistant components and higher duty class for foundries, forging shops and continuous production.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Single girder or double girder: which one do I need?

For light to medium loads and normal hook height a single girder crane is more economical. For heavier loads, long spans or where maximum hook height is needed, a double girder crane is the better choice. Share your load, span and height and we will advise.

Can you install a crane in an existing building?

Yes. We check the building columns and runway beams, suggest the right crane type and handle erection and commissioning at site.

Do you repair old cranes?

Yes. We repair and overhaul old EOT cranes and can upgrade them with new hoists, electricals, remote control and safety devices.
