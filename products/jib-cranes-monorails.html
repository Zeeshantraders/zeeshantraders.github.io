Overview

About Jib Cranes & Monorails

A jib crane has a horizontal arm (the jib) that swings around a pillar or a wall bracket, with a hoist running along the arm. It covers a circular area and is perfect for lifting at a single machine, workbench or loading point.

A monorail is a single beam path along which a hoist travels in a straight line or along curves. It is a simple way to move loads from one point to another, for example from a store to a machine or from a truck to a platform.

These light cranes are quick to install, low in cost and reduce manual handling and worker injuries.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a pillar jib crane need a foundation?

Yes. A free-standing pillar needs a concrete foundation sized for the load and arm length. We provide the foundation details.

Can a monorail be curved?

Yes. Monorail beams can be bent to follow a route around the building.
