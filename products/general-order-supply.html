Overview

About General Order Supply

Along with lifting equipment, Zeeshan Traders works as a general order supplier. Government departments and organisations often need a wide range of items under one tender, and we handle the sourcing, quality and delivery.

Being registered with FBR, Sales Tax and the Lahore Chamber of Commerce, and as a registered importer, we can supply both local and imported items with proper invoices and documentation.
