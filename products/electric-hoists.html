Overview

About Electric Hoists

An electric hoist is the lifting unit of most cranes. A motor drives a drum (wire rope hoist) or a load sprocket (chain hoist) through a gearbox, and a brake holds the load securely when the motor stops.

Wire rope hoists are used for heavier loads, higher lifting heights and faster speeds. Electric chain hoists are compact and economical for lighter loads.

We supply new hoists for new cranes, replacement hoists for old cranes, and spare parts and repair for all common types of hoist.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Wire rope hoist or chain hoist?

For loads above about 5 ton, high lifts or heavy use, a wire rope hoist is better. For lighter loads and occasional use, a chain hoist is compact and economical.

Can you replace the hoist on my old crane?

Yes. We supply a matching hoist and handle the fitting and electrical connection.
