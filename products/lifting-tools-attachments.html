Overview

About Lifting Tools & Attachments

Below-the-hook attachments make it possible to lift loads that cannot be slung easily, such as steel plates, drums, long bars or large machines. They save time and make the lift safer and more controlled.

Jacks and pallet trucks are used to raise and move loads at ground level, for machine shifting and store handling.

Range

Types We Supply

Plate Lifting Clamp

Grips steel plates for vertical or horizontal lifting without slings.

Lifting Magnet

Permanent magnet lifter with on/off lever for steel plates, blocks and round bars; no power needed.

Spreader & Lifting Beam

Spreads the lifting points to lift long or wide loads without crushing them.

Drum Lifter

Clamp for safely lifting standard steel drums.

Jacks & Pallet Trucks

Hydraulic bottle jacks, toe jacks, mechanical jacks and hand pallet trucks for ground level handling.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a magnet lift any steel?

Lifting magnets are rated for flat, clean, thick steel. Capacity reduces for thin, rough or round material. We help you select the right size.

Do you make custom spreader beams?

Yes. Spreader and lifting beams are fabricated to your load, length and lifting points.
