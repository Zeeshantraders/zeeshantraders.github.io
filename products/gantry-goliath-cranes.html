Overview

About Gantry & Goliath Cranes

A gantry crane works like an overhead crane, but instead of running on the building it stands on its own legs. The legs travel on rails laid on the ground or on wheels, which makes gantry cranes ideal for open yards, stockyards and loading bays.

Large heavy-duty gantry cranes are often called goliath cranes. A semi-gantry has one leg on the ground and the other side running on a rail fixed to a building or wall.

Zeeshan Traders supplies gantry cranes from small portable A-frame gantries used in workshops to large rail-mounted goliath cranes for heavy industry and infrastructure projects.

Range

Types We Supply

Single Girder Gantry Crane

One girder on two legs with an electric hoist. Simple and economical for light and medium loads in yards.

Double Girder Gantry Crane

Two girders with a crab trolley for heavy loads, long spans and high lifting height.

Semi-Gantry Crane

One side on legs, the other side on a building rail. Saves cost where one wall can carry the load.

Goliath Crane

Heavy-duty rail-mounted gantry for steel yards, precast plants, bridge and dam projects.

Portable / Mobile A-Frame Gantry

Light steel or aluminium frame on castors. Moves around the workshop and needs no foundation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a gantry crane and an overhead crane?

An overhead crane runs on rails fixed to the building. A gantry crane stands on its own legs and runs on the ground, so it does not need a building to support it.

Do you supply small gantries for workshops?

Yes. Portable A-frame gantries with chain blocks or electric hoists are available for light lifting in workshops and stores.

Can a gantry crane work in the open?

Yes. Outdoor gantries are supplied with weatherproof electricals and storm locks for safe operation in wind and rain.
