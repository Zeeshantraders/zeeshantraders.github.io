Overview

About Rigging Hardware

Rigging hardware is the set of fittings that join slings, ropes and loads together. Every part in a lifting arrangement must be rated for the load, so good quality, correctly marked hardware is essential for safe lifting.

We stock galvanized and alloy steel rigging hardware in a wide range of sizes, for lifting, tensioning and general rigging.

Range

Types We Supply

D & Bow Shackles

D shackles for straight pulls; bow shackles for multi-leg slings and angled loads. Screw pin and bolt type.

Eye, Swivel & Self-Locking Hooks

Forged hooks with safety latch; self-locking hooks close automatically under load.

Eye Bolts, Eye Nuts & Turnbuckles

Lifting points on machines and moulds; turnbuckles for tensioning ropes and stays.

Wire Rope Clips & Thimbles

For making eyes and terminations in wire rope; thimbles protect the eye from wear.

Master Links, Swivels & Pulley Blocks

Master links for sling assemblies, swivels to stop twisting, and snatch / pulley blocks to change rope direction.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use any shackle for lifting?

Only shackles marked with a working load limit should be used for lifting. Unmarked hardware is for general use only.

Do you supply in bulk for tenders?

Yes. We supply rigging hardware in bulk quantities as per tender specification.
