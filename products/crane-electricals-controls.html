Overview

About Crane Electricals & Controls

The electrical system controls every movement of a crane and protects it from overload and over-travel. Faulty electricals are one of the main causes of crane breakdowns.

We supply and install complete crane control panels, upgrade old cranes to radio remote control and variable frequency drives, and repair motors and electrical faults.

Range

Types We Supply

Pendant Push Button Station

Hand-held control hanging from the crane, with emergency stop.

Radio Remote Control

Wireless control so the operator can stand at a safe distance with a clear view.

Control Panels, Contactors & VFDs

Complete crane panels; VFDs give smooth acceleration and reduce load swing.

Limit Switches & Overload Limiters

Stop hoisting and travel at safe limits and prevent lifting more than rated load.

Festoon Systems & Conductor Bars (DSL)

Supply power to the moving crane and trolley safely.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my old crane be upgraded to remote control?

Yes. Almost any crane can be converted to radio remote control, usually without changing the motors.

What is the benefit of a VFD?

A VFD starts and stops motors smoothly, reduces load swing, saves wear on gearboxes and brakes, and gives precise speed control.
