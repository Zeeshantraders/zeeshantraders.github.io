Overview

About Winches

A winch winds a wire rope onto a drum to pull or lift a load. Winches are used where a load must be hauled along the ground, pulled up a slope, or lifted from a fixed point.

Electric winches suit regular heavy work, hand winches are used for light or occasional tasks, and hydraulic winches are used on vehicles and machines that already have a hydraulic system.

We supply winches complete with wire rope and accessories, and repair and rewind existing winches.

Rotating drum used for pulling rope and cable, common in cable laying and utilities.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a pulling winch for lifting?

Only winches designed and rated for lifting should be used to lift loads. Pulling winches are for horizontal hauling. Tell us your use and we will supply the right type.

Do you supply the wire rope with the winch?

Yes. The winch can be supplied with matching wire rope, hook and accessories.
