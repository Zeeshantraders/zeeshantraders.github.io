Overview

About Lifting Slings

A sling connects the load to the crane hook. The right sling depends on the weight and shape of the load, the surface finish, and the conditions where it is used.

Wire rope slings are strong and durable, chain slings handle rough and hot conditions, and polyester webbing and round slings protect delicate or painted surfaces.

We supply ready slings and also fabricate wire rope slings to the required length and capacity.

Range

Types We Supply

Wire Rope Sling

Steel rope with eyes, ferrule-pressed or hand spliced. Strong and long lasting for general lifting.

Alloy Chain Sling

Grade 80 or grade 100 alloy chain. Tough, adjustable and suitable for rough and hot loads.

Polyester Webbing Sling

Flat belt sling that grips the load and protects finished surfaces. Colour coded by capacity.

Round Sling

Endless polyester sling, soft and flexible, for delicate and odd-shaped loads.

Multi-Leg Sling Assembly

Two, three or four leg slings with a master link for balanced lifting of large loads.

Features

Key Features

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know a sling's capacity?

Every sling carries a tag showing its safe working load. Webbing slings also follow a standard colour code. Never use a sling without a readable tag.

Can you make slings to my length?

Yes. Wire rope slings are fabricated to the length, diameter and end fittings you need.
