Overview

About Safety & Testing Equipment

Safe lifting needs two things: knowing the exact weight of the load, and protecting the people who work around cranes and at height.

We supply weighing and testing equipment for load checks, and a full range of personal protective equipment (PPE) for crane operators, riggers and maintenance teams.

Range

Types We Supply

Load Cells & Dynamometers

Measure tension and load during lifting and during crane load tests.

Crane Scales

Hang from the hook to weigh loads while lifting, with digital display.

Full Body Harness & Fall Arresters

Fall protection for work at height on cranes, towers and structures.

Safety Helmets, Gloves & PPE

Helmets, gloves, safety shoes, goggles and high-visibility vests.

Warning Lights & Alarms

Flashing lights and sirens that warn people when the crane is moving.
