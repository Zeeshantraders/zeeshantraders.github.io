Overview

About Steel Wire Rope

Steel wire rope is made of many steel wires twisted into strands, and strands twisted around a core. The construction (number of strands and wires) decides how flexible and how wear-resistant the rope is.

Choosing the right rope matters for safety and rope life. A crane needs a flexible rope that runs smoothly over sheaves; a hoist with a single fall may need a rotation resistant rope so the load does not spin.

Zeeshan Traders supplies wire rope cut to the required length, and replaces worn ropes on cranes, hoists and winches.

Range

Types We Supply

6x19 Construction

Good balance of strength and abrasion resistance for general purpose and winch use.

6x36 Construction

More flexible rope for cranes and hoists running over sheaves and drums.

8-Strand Rope

Higher flexibility and better fatigue life for modern cranes and hoists.

Non-Rotating / Rotation Resistant Rope

Reduces spinning of the load on high lifts and single-fall hoists.

Galvanized & Ungalvanized Rope

Galvanized rope resists corrosion for outdoor and marine use; ungalvanized (bright) rope is lubricated for indoor use.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When should crane wire rope be replaced?

Wire rope should be replaced when inspection shows broken wires beyond limits, reduced diameter, kinks, crushing, corrosion or bird-caging. We can inspect and replace your rope.

What information do you need to quote?

Rope diameter, construction, core type, length, lay direction and finish. If you do not know, share the crane or hoist model and we will identify the right rope.
