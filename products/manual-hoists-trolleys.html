Overview

About Manual Hoists & Trolleys

Manual hoists lift heavy loads by hand through a gear reduction, so one person can lift a load many times their own strength. They need no electricity and are used everywhere from workshops to construction sites.

A chain pulley block lifts vertically using a hand chain. A lever hoist (ratchet pull lift) works with a lever and can lift, pull and tension loads in any direction.

Beam trolleys and clamps let you hang a hoist from a steel beam and move it along the beam.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better, chain block or lever hoist?

A chain block is better for straight vertical lifting. A lever hoist is better for pulling, tensioning and work in tight spaces or at angles.

Can you supply longer lifting heights?

Yes. Chain blocks can be supplied with extra chain for longer lifts.
