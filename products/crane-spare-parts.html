Overview

About Crane Spare Parts

A crane stays safe and productive only when worn parts are replaced in time. Wheels, brakes, ropes, sheaves and gearboxes wear with use and must be replaced to avoid breakdowns and accidents.

Zeeshan Traders supplies spare parts for most common crane and hoist makes, sources hard-to-find parts through import, and can manufacture parts to sample or drawing.

Range

Types We Supply

Wheels, End Carriages & Crane Rails

Forged and cast wheels, complete end carriages and crane rails with clips.

Gear Boxes, Brakes & Thrusters

Hoist and travel gearboxes, disc and drum brakes, electro-hydraulic thrusters.

Rope Drums, Sheaves & Hook Blocks

Grooved drums, rope sheaves and complete bottom hook blocks.

Crane Motors & Couplings

Hoist and travel motors, brake motors, flexible and gear couplings.

Buffers & Rail Clamps

Rubber and polyurethane buffers, end stoppers and rail clamps.
